## Identity
Kirjane Labs is a Bohol-based software engineering business (Kirjane IT Solutions). The website must demonstrate enterprise-grade workmanship through the *quality of its own interface*: distinctive editorial design, sharp typography, disciplined grids, responsive execution and cohesive motion.

## Brand
- **Assets:** inspect `public/brand/` real official logo and chameleon variations, including color, green and white marks.
- **Palette:** existing dark green, green, yellow-green, mist white, white. Exact official HEX are **not approved/verified** in this repo yet; derive and verify before locking.
- **Tone:** confident, precise, business-readable, no internal technical theater or unsubstantiated claims.
- **Typography:** editorial display with a complementary practical body face. Antigravity to document the selected licensed font family, line lengths, fluid sizing, and alignment system after rendered comparison.
- **Shape:** premium architectural grid, balanced negative space, restrained borders, no generic pill/card-wall template or arbitrary 01/02 labels.
- **Motion:** coherent choreography, not scattered appearance animations. Motion choreography draft is in `docs/KIRJANE-MOTION-STORYBOARD-R1.md`.

## Layout intentions
- Hero: striking, legible offer and meaningful CTA, with official branding as subtle depth. Ensure direct intelligibility to a nontechnical client.
- Services: purposeful differentiated capabilities, not repeated generic rectangles.
- Projects: authentic product and project demonstrations with honest maturity and status; examples are Salryn, Scoryn and Talibon Intra-Office.
- About/team: full-bleed split composition (portrait stage left, concise detail right) and intentionally separate mobile layout. Seven members, no fake quotes.
- Process: five-stage interactive story with reversible scroll progress and direct keyboard/touch selection.
- Contact: obvious communication path with tested user feedback and no unverified links.
