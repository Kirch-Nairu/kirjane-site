## New thesis: ENGINEERED REALITY
Keep the existing R3 Living Topography / Engineered Clarity story. Evolve its representation from sci-fi decoration into functional visual mechanisms a buyer can understand.
A beautiful object is unacceptable when it doesn't explain a system. Every visual must answer: what is it, what changed, why did it change, what remains verifiable? Assign light and color to real states rather than ambient neon.
Offer three viewing distances: 3-second silhouette / 10-second meaning / 30-second interaction. On a paused frame, the composition must still teach.

## Form language
1. STRUCTURE: engineered joints, nested components, legible interfaces, dimensioned edge breaks; not default cubes/toruses/rings.
2. DATA: routed tracks and ports, with source and destination; not unsourced particles or meaningless orbital noise.
3. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY: approvals, exceptions, escalation and audit trails as distinctive mechanisms.
4. MATERIAL: ceramic signals record stability, titanium signals engineering, glass limited to inspection/exposure, soft cloth/paper/stone for warm editorial contrast. Do not apply glass to every object.
5. RHYTHM: reveal, deliberate hold, consequence, recovery. Motion must show causality and reverse cleanly; avoid spinning continuously.
6. SPACE: one focal object with intentional silhouette, asymmetrical white space for typography, designed negative space. Larger objects when focal; fewer accessories.

## Color and lighting study
Official green palette and logo geometry are locked, as is the canonical 2D Drive chameleon. The expanded color tokens are experimental: see COLOR_MATERIAL_STUDY.json.
Blue/cobalt = software structures; amber = approvals/automation; cyan = integrations; graphite/titanium = infrastructure; copper = interruption; violet = finance or specialized operation. Consistent semantic accents are easier to read than one green stage.
Alternate warm light stages and graphite technical stages instead of an endless pine-colored corridor. Use eye-level/low-key light with specific key, fill and rim; no fully black object merging into same-hue background.

## Three critical examples
Services: four true distinct modeled mechanisms, NOT four poses of one cluster: a record-vault with custody layers; an approval-gate conveyor with exception; an interoperability port bridge; a continuity station with isolated power/boundary.
Process: five frames of one physically connected path, with trace -> separation -> construction -> verification failure/retry -> durable handover. Blender models the stage and lighting; accessible HTML/SVG supplies labels and precise interaction.
Salryn: ONE identifiable eight-engine physical architecture spanning hero/workspaces/industries/configurator. Thin rings are secondary cues. Security and Infrastructure remain invariant and cannot be disabled.

## Art-director vetoes
REJECT: random green rings, repetitive floating slabs, arbitrary glowing specks, generic neon tunnel, empty dramatic stage, visual complexity without meaning, brand substitution, tiny object within a giant unused panel, illegible text, fake real-business claims.
PREFER: strong engineering silhouette, intentional crop, restrained palette, physically believable highlights, differentiated objects, visible consequences, accessible captions, human-scale editorial balance.
