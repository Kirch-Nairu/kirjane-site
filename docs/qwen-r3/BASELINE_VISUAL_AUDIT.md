## Actual desktop capture findings

- **03 Home services:** the upper section has a large, flat pale panel; translucent block art sits small relative to the stage; the dark right-side content panel renders dark text on a dark background and cannot be read. This is a CRITICAL information-hierarchy and accessibility failure. Replace the scene and its structural proportions, not just the background or text color.
- **04 Home Process:** luminous topology lines themselves communicate energy, but they terminate visually at a rigid pale rectangle/card stage, making the section feel assembled from two separate art systems. Keep meaningful stages; redesign the whole surrounding narrative.
- **08 Salryn industry:** the white/cream square radial picture contrasts strongly with the dark green container and looks like a presentation diagram pasted into a 3D software story. The industry paragraph and selection have minimal spatial consequence.
- **09 Salryn capability:** the eight-engine list is functionally dense and tiny. The headless capture contains an explicit static 'SALRYN' optical fallback rather than hardware R3F nodes; this must be interpreted honestly. The subject scale, readability and configurator relationship need redesign on actual hardware.
- **10 Salryn offline:** the central SVG sits small on a dark rectangle amid unused green space; connection lines and operational semantics cannot be inspected at a glance. Needs a stateful operational simulation with large legible node labels.

## Actual mobile 390×844 capture findings

- **m03 Home services:** software artwork is stacked above a near-black explanatory rail with nearly unreadable dark text. A service visitor cannot grasp the selected mode. **P0 accessibility + composition issue.**
- **m04 Process:** the stage strip becomes a crowded horizontal control, with text and content cards too constrained for a guided five-step story. Need mobile-specific step progression rather than squeezing desktop process layout.
- **m08 Salryn industry:** the available native selector is readable and should be retained as a functional input; white diagram remains visually generic and output is mostly static copy. Build a distinct small-screen scene and actual industry workflow.
- **m09 Salryn engine activation:** the capability list dominates the phone screen; the 3D/fallback object is not even visible within the selected first viewport. The selected engines and core visual need a clear mobile hierarchy and separate scannable view.
- **m10 Offline-first:** the schematic is a tiny dark screenshot with labels impossible to read at phone size. Scene state controls exist, but a viewer cannot understand the actual path from the visual. Replace with a mobile-first state progression.
