# Kirjane Labs — R2 Content & Asset Provenance Policy

**Planning status. Publication of any new claim remains subject to source verification.**

## Company identity and voice
Use `Kirjane Labs` visibly; current project copy identifies legal name `Kirjane IT Solutions`, brand motto `Adapt. Automate. Evolve.`, and operations based in Bohol. Verify official legal status and exact contact channels before public launch. Distinguish marketing promise from measured capability.

Tone: plain English, confident and useful for LGU officials, business owners and procurement contacts. No copied fake customer testimonials, fabricated company metrics, pseudo-scientific interface text, lorem ipsum, or "AI generated"-sounding slogans.

## Featured work — proof required
- **Salryn:** The actual product and its branches have their own repo, tests, acceptance gates and pilot scope. Website content describing accounting ledger, retail, cafe, pharmacy, hardware, multi-branch etc must each tie to exact accepted/QA evidence. Do not infer implementation from this website's `products.ts` status labels. Approved screenshots should be genuine, redacted of client data.
- **Scoryn:** Verify events, scores, judging functions, permission to reference organizers, and available real screenshots before printing "trusted by" language. A past UI claim is not evidence of a specific event being served.
- **Talibon Intra-Office:** Verify current project status and permission to publish municipality references/screenshots, especially where the system is not deployed for public use. Describe under-development work accurately.
- **Services:** The firm may describe offered skills (backend, security, hosting, websites), but do not invent SLAs, certifications or guaranteed uptime. Proposals and implementations are distinct.

## Team
Keep the seven-person roster if confirmed, but verify role/titles and any photo consent individually. No made-up quotations, credentials or founder titles. When photos are missing, use editorial typographic profiles, **not fake portraits**.

## Approved media registry
Every asset: canonical filename, rights holder/source, consent or publication approval, attribution if needed, aspect ratios, alt text, and availability status. Use existing official `public/brand/*` only after verifying which files are actually clean transparent marks vs embedded marketing card images.

## Inquiries
An email composer based on `mailto:` is not a delivered form. Label it `Open email draft`, with copy-to-clipboard fallback. Official Facebook URL must be verified and reachable; do not claim a "sent" event without server acknowledgment.
